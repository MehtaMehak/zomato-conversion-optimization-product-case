# 📋 Survey Used in This Project

This project includes primary research to understand user behavior on food delivery apps.

## 🎯 Purpose
To analyze why users open the app but do not complete orders.

## 🔗 Survey Link
https://forms.gle/f6A55BqpstAYoX3p7

## 🧠 What We Asked

- Why do you open Zomato?
- How often do you order food?
- Do you compare multiple restaurants before ordering?
- Have you ever added items to cart but not ordered?
- What was the reason for not completing the order?
- Do delivery charges affect your decision?
- Do you experience delivery delays?
- Do you use other apps like Swiggy?

## 💡 Why This Matters

This survey helped identify:
- High intent vs low conversion
- Pricing sensitivity
- Decision fatigue
- Trust & switching behavior
